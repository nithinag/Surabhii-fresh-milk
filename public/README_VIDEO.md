# Hero Background Video Setup

To add a background video to the hero section:

1. **Add your video file** to this `public` folder:
   - Recommended: `hero-video.mp4` (MP4 format, H.264 codec)
   - Optional: `hero-video.webm` (WebM format for better compression)

2. **Video Specifications:**
   - Format: MP4 (H.264) or WebM
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
   - Duration: 10-30 seconds (will loop)
   - File size: Keep under 5MB for best performance
   - Content: Farm scenes, cows, milk production, or peaceful dairy farm imagery

3. **Video Tips:**
   - Use a video that's not too busy (subtle movement works best)
   - Ensure good lighting and professional quality
   - Consider adding a subtle overlay in post-production
   - Test on mobile devices to ensure it doesn't impact performance

4. **If you don't have a video:**
   - The hero section will fall back to a cream-colored background
   - You can use stock video from sites like Pexels, Pixabay, or Unsplash
   - Search for: "dairy farm", "cow milk", "organic farm", "fresh milk"

5. **Example video sources:**
   - Pexels: https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/dairy%20farm/
   - Pixabay: https://pixabay.com/videos/search/dairy%20farm/
   - Unsplash: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/dairy-farm

The video will automatically:
- Play on load (muted and looped)
- Cover the full hero section
- Have a cream overlay for text readability
- Fall back gracefully if video fails to load
